## 8 · WHO THIS IS FOR, AND WHO IT IS NOT FOR

# This is not for everyone, and we would rather say so now.

**It is for you if:**

- The back, neck or knee has been going on for months rather than days
- You sit for a living, at a desk, hybrid or fully at home
- You have already tried physiotherapy, painkillers, a better chair or a stretch routine, and it came back
- You can give twenty to thirty minutes most days and stay with it for months rather than weeks
- You want the toolkit to manage it yourself, rather than to keep paying someone else to manage it

**It is not for you if:**

- You are in acute post-injury or post-surgical recovery and have not been cleared to move
- You have a diagnosed condition where your clinician has said movement is not advised. Your doctor decides that, not us.
- You are looking for relief this week
- You need a fully personalised rehabilitation plan rather than a live group

Nobody is promised relief on day one. The case files say most people notice the first real change around the third session, and it builds from there. If that pace is not what you are looking for, please do not spend the £6.

## 13 · WHY THIS WORKS

# Most back programmes start by strengthening the back. That is why it comes back.

The Inner Brace Method works on where the load is landing, before it goes anywhere near the part that hurts.

> Sitting for nine hours a day does not just make somebody stiff. It changes which muscles fire, how they breathe, and where the load lands when they stand up. **Until those three things change, stretching the sore part does not touch the thing sending the signal.** That is why the massage helped for an afternoon and not for a week, and why it came back four months after the physiotherapy ended.

**01 · UNLOAD. Take the weight off, so the body stops defending.**
Props, walls and straps take the load off, which is what tells the nervous system it is safe to let go.

**02 · BRACE. Wake the brace you already have.**
The transverse abdominis, the multifidus and the pelvic floor. Your own internal brace, quietly off duty for years.

**03 · MOVE. Put it back into ordinary life.**
Hips and shoulders moving freely over a supported spine. Standing work, and the movements that had become a decision.

The order cannot be skipped. Going straight to strengthening a back that is already guarding is the most common thing people do on their own, and it is the reason it gets worse. Props are not a beginner's crutch either. They are how you tell a nervous system it is allowed to let go.

## 15 · FAIR QUESTIONS

**"I have tried yoga. It did nothing, or it made it worse."**
Probably a flow class, and a flow class is not sequenced for a back that has been compensating for two years. The wrong shape on an irritated spine sets it backwards. This is a sequenced method with a teacher watching, and the first phase deliberately does not go near the part that hurts.

**"The NHS and my physio already do this."**
This is not a replacement for physiotherapy, for your GP, or for any clinical care you are having. It sits alongside them. What five live days add is a specific sequence, a teacher watching how you move in real time, and a method you keep and run yourself afterwards.

**"My employer gives me an app for this for free."**
Then use it. What it cannot do is watch you. App content is asynchronous and nobody is correcting the way you are actually performing the movement. We are not a regulated medical device and we do not claim to be one. We are a live teacher, in a room, five days running.

**"What if I cannot make the live session?"**
Every session runs twice, morning and evening, with the same content, so you pick one. If you miss one entirely there is a replay for 24 hours. It expires after that, because the useful part is the correction and the questions, and an open replay library turns this into content nobody watches.

**"Do I need to be flexible, or buy anything?"**
No, and no. A mat, a wall, a belt or a dressing gown cord, and a couple of cushions. The one-page list is sent when you register. Props are how a body that cannot yet do the shape gets the benefit of it safely.

**"Is five days really going to change anything?"**
Honestly: five days is not where the change lives, and we would rather tell you that now than sell you something else. The case files say the first real change usually lands around the third session, three weeks for morning stiffness, eight weeks for a full working day seated. What the five days give you is your own number on Monday and again on Thursday, and a clear read on whether this is worth your time.
