### Change 21

**NEW IN v2:**

**The before-and-after imagery can run, but only one kind of it.** The updated strategy asks for "a simple side-by-side of a forward fold or a seated posture". Appendix A still rules out before-and-after body shots implying medical improvement. These can coexist, because a range-of-movement side-by-side shows a joint angle and a before-and-after shows a body, but the distinction lives entirely in the execution. Four conditions, and if any one fails it becomes the thing the pre-flight rules out. Same clothing, same camera position, same lighting, both frames dated. Fully clothed, loose kit, the joint as the subject rather than the torso. The caption names the movement and the case-file timeline, never a health outcome, so "Forward fold, week one and week twelve" runs and "twelve weeks to a better back" does not. And the results-vary note sits next to the image rather than in the footer.
